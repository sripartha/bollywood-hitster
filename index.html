🎲 How We Play BollyGood

  1. Setup: Split into two teams (or more). Deal each team 1 face-up starting card to begin their timeline, plus 2 BollyGood Tokens 🪙.
  2. Opposing Team Scans & Passes the Card: On Team A's turn, Team B draws a card, scans the QR code on the iPhone, and can quietly pass the data side of the card around their own team while the Bollywood hook plays over Bluetooth!
  3. Step 1 — Qualify (Film + Song, or "No Film" + Artist + Song):
    • If it is a Film Song: The guessing team must name both the Film Name and the Song Title (naming the singer/artist gives no extra mileage on film songs).
    • If it is a No-Film (Indipop) Song: The guessing team must say "No Film" plus the Artist Name and Song Title!
    • Getting Step 1 right qualifies the team to guess the proper Year Window!
  4. Step 2 — Place in the Proper Year Window:
    • Once qualified, the team places the card into the proper chronological Year Window among the cards already in their timeline (before, between, or after existing cards; matching the exact year of an adjacent card is valid on either side).
  5. Reveal & Score:
    • Correct Year Window: Keep the card face-up in your timeline!
    • Bonus Token 🪙: Correctly naming the Movie / Song / Singer earns +1 BollyGood Token.
    • Steal ("BollyGood!"): If the guessing team seems unsure, the opposing team can spend 1 Token to place their own bet on a different slot—if the guessing team is wrong and the steal slot is right, the opposing team steals the card!
  6. Winning: First team to collect 10 cards in their chronological timeline wins!
